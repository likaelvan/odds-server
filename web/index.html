    Reading the numbers
    • Implied probability is 1 ÷ decimal odds. Odds of 2.00 imply a 50% chance.
    • Overround: a single bookmaker's two probabilities add up to more than 100%. The extra is its margin.
    • Pricing gap: when the best price for each player comes from different bookmakers and the two add up to under 100%, the markets disagree. Allocating across both prices returns the same whichever player wins.
    • Market view removes each bookmaker's margin and averages them, giving a consensus win probability.
    • Odds move quickly, and bookmakers handle mid-match retirements differently, so a gap on paper may not hold up for long.
